About

Why Watermelon?

Watermelon is not just a sweet snack; it’s a hydrating powerhouse packed with vitamins, antioxidants, and a story that spans centuries.

Originating in Africa, watermelon traveled along ancient trade routes to become a global staple. Its high water content (about 92%) makes it the perfect refresher on hot days.

Beyond taste, the fruit contributes lycopene, vitamin C, and potassium, supporting skin health and heart function.
